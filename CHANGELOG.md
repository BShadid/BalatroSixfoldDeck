# Changelog

All notable changes to the Sixfold Deck are listed here. Versions follow the `version`
field in `SixfoldDeck.json`; each release's notes are taken from its section below.

## [0.9.3] - 2026-11-13

First public release.

### Added
- **Sixfold Deck:** play and discard up to 6 cards; −1 hand every round.
- **Nine new 6-card poker hands:** Three Pair, Long Straight, Big Flush, Double Trips,
  Mansion and Long Straight Flush, plus the secret Six of a Kind, Flush Mansion and Flush Six.
- **Nine new planet cards** (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Triton, Haumea,
  Makemake, Sedna) with original art.
- **Growing X-Mult:** each 6-card hand played adds X0.1 Mult to 6-card hands for the rest
  of the run, shown on a badge above the draw pile.
- **Cash-out bonus:** $2 per 6-card hand played that round.
- **Joker changes in Sixfold runs:** Superposition, Seance (with sound effects), Runner,
  Spare Trousers, Faceless Joker and To Do List gain 6-card upgrades, each with a tooltip.
- Original card back art.

### Changed
- In Sixfold runs, a Straight Flush must be made from the same five cards.
